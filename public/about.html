ABOUT SSV COMMERCIAL SERVICES & INFRA

SSV Commercial Services & Infra Private Limited delivers professional security, housekeeping, and infrastructure solutions designed to support safer, cleaner, and better-managed environments.

OPERATIONAL ETHOS

SAFETY AND SECURITY AS FUNDAMENTAL OPERATIONAL PILLARS

SSV recognizes that safety and security are fundamental to seamless operations. The company provides security and housekeeping solutions supported by trained personnel, modern technology, professional practices, and customer-centric service.

Alongside our facilities divisions, SSV's civil and turnkey infrastructure capability executes complex utility shifting, road building, and turnkey civil assignments across Maharashtra.
