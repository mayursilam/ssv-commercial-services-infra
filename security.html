SECURITY & MANNED GUARDING

Disciplined physical protection, centralized electronic CCTV surveillance, and executive security frameworks tailored for high-asset properties.

DEFENSE FRAMEWORKS

COMPREHENSIVE SECURITY MODULES

Seven specialized security divisions operating under rigorous SOPs.

01

Manned Guarding

Vigilant on-ground physical protection

Disciplined, uniformed security guards trained in access control, patrol procedures, incident logging, and gatekeeping for commercial, industrial, and residential properties.

  • 24/7 Static & roving patrols
  • Stringent background verification
  • Visitor & vehicle registration logging
  • Emergency escalation readiness
02

Corporate Security

Executive workplace protection & access management

Specialized security personnel tailored for corporate headquarters, tech parks, and commercial towers requiring professional presentation and discreet vigilance.

  • Front-desk concierge & security liaison
  • Badge management & turnstile monitoring
  • Confidential facility asset protection
  • After-hours floor security checks
03

Crowd Management & Events

Strategic crowd control for high-footfall events

Operational protocols designed to prevent bottlenecks, ensure structured queuing, and maintain public safety during exhibitions, summits, and large gatherings.

  • Perimeter barricade coordination
  • Entry/exit throughput optimization
  • Emergency evacuation route clearance
  • Conflict de-escalation tactics
04

VIP Protection

Close protection for executives & dignitaries

Discreet personal protection officers trained in route surveying, close-quarter security escort, risk avoidance, and secure transit coordination.

  • Advance venue reconnaissance
  • Close protection escort details
  • Secure route planning & transit safety
  • Discreet, high-readiness presence
05

CCTV Surveillance & Monitoring

Continuous electronic video oversight

Centralized control-room monitoring and live video oversight to detect anomalies, perimeter breaches, and operational hazards in real time.

  • 24/7 Dedicated command-center feeds
  • Incident playback & forensic archiving
  • Multi-camera zone coverage audits
  • Rapid dispatch alert integration
06

Access Control Systems

Strict authorization & visitor management

Physical and electronic access enforcement restricting unauthorized entry, managing delivery vehicles, and securing restricted zones.

  • Biometric & RFID card verification
  • Boom barrier & turnstile supervision
  • Material in/out gate pass tracking
  • Visitor identity validation
07

Fire Safety & Emergency Response

Proactive hazard prevention & rapid evacuation

Security officers trained in basic firefighting, emergency response drills, fire extinguisher operation, and rapid building evacuation protocols.

  • First-responder fire extinguishing readiness
  • Emergency evacuation coordination
  • Fire exit & hazard obstruction checks
  • Coordination with municipal emergency teams
